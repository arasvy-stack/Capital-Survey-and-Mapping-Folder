Surveying Services

Field measurement and control for design, construction and records.

Services marked draft are proposed offerings pending company confirmation.

Topographic surveys

Purpose: record ground levels and features for design.

Applications: site planning, drainage, road design.

Deliverables: CAD plan, contours, spot levels, point file.

Construction setting out

Purpose: place design geometry accurately on site.

Applications: foundations, roads, utilities, structures.

Deliverables: marked points, setting-out sheets, check records.

As-built surveys

Purpose: verify what was built against design.

Applications: handover, claims, record drawings.

Deliverables: as-built drawings, deviation tables.

Control surveys & benchmark verification

Purpose: establish and check the reference framework.

Applications: all projects needing consistent coordinates and levels.

Deliverables: control coordinate list, level checks, report.

GNSS / RTK surveys

Purpose: fast positioning with base and rover receivers.

Applications: open-sky topography, stakeout, control densification.

Deliverables: coordinate files, quality summaries.

Static GNSS & network adjustment draft

Purpose: higher-confidence control from longer observations.

Applications: project control networks.

Deliverables: adjusted coordinates, adjustment report.

Route & corridor surveys

Purpose: map a linear strip for alignment design.

Applications: roads, pipelines, OHTL, rail.

Deliverables: corridor plan, longitudinal profile, cross-sections.

Earthwork & stockpile volume surveys

Purpose: measure volumes from surveyed surfaces.

Applications: cut and fill, material stockpiles, progress claims.

Deliverables: surface model, volume report.

Accuracy depends on equipment, site conditions, observation procedures and quality control. Project-specific tolerances are agreed in the quotation.

Instrument Types & Applications

General descriptions of equipment used in surveying. This is not an inventory of our own equipment.

Total stations

Measures: horizontal and vertical angles and distances.

Used for: setting out, detail surveys, as-builts.

Components: telescope, EDM, angle encoders, tribrach, prism.

Affects results: setup quality, atmosphere, target centring, calibration.

Outputs: coordinates and field data files.

GNSS receivers (base / rover)

Measures: satellite signals to compute position.

Used for: control, topography, stakeout.

Components: receiver, antenna, pole, controller, radio or network link.

Affects results: sky view, multipath, observation time, corrections.

Outputs: coordinates with quality indicators.

Digital and automatic levels

Measures: height differences.

Used for: benchmarks, level runs, floor and formation levels.

Components: level, tripod, staff.

Affects results: collimation, staff verticality, sight lengths, loop closure.

Outputs: reduced levels and misclosure checks.

Accessories

Prisms, tripods, poles and staffs link the instrument to the ground point. Their condition and bubble adjustment directly affect results.
